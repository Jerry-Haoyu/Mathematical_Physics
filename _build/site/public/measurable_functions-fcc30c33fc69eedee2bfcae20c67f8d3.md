# Measurable Functions 

:::{exercise}
:label: 
Let $f : X \to \overline{\mathbb{R}}$. Prove that the following are equivalent: 
(a) $\{x : f(x) > a\} \in \mathcal{M}$ for every $a \in \mathbb{R}$; (b) $\{x : f(x) \ge a\} \in \mathcal{M}$ for every $a$; (c) $f^{-1}(U) \in \mathcal{M}$ for every open $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}$. Then prove that if $f, g$ are measurable and real-valued, so are $f + g$ and $f g$
:::
