Mathematical Physics and Computation

Why Mathematical Physics and Computation¶

A traditional course in mathematical physics provides solid mathematical background for physics research. However, apart from a few fields that deals with pure theory, most theoretical and applied physical sciences involves numerical computing extensively. Moreover, the scope of the two fields naturally overlap and has a complementary role. To be more explicit, I happen to share a point of view described below:

Definition 1 (What is Mathematical Physics and Scientific Computation)

  1. Mathematical physics is a collection of frameworks that describe Physics.

  2. Scientific computation gives the pratical algorithm that solves a physical problem in these frameworks.

So often mathematical phsyics and scientfic computation are two algorthmic black boxes researchers use in combination to understand the world:

  1. Given a problem Π\PiΠ, mathematical physics produce a representation of the problem in a set of frameworks [Π]F[\Pi]_{F}[Π]F​ where FFF is relevant frameworks used.

  2. [Π]F[\Pi]_{F}[Π]F​ is passed to the “scientific computation” box to work up numerical results.
